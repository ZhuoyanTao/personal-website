I'm Zhuoyan Tao, Terry to friends and colleagues. I'm a master's student in CMU's Intelligent Information Systems program at the Language Technologies Institute, advised by Prof. Shinji Watanabe at WavLab.

My work sits at the boundary of signal processing, machine learning, and linguistics, and keeps circling one question: how meaning is carried in speech beyond just the words. Right now I'm the lead student researcher on target-speaker speech restoration with Amazon AGI, recovering one speaker from a damaged phone call given only a short sample of their voice.

Before CMU I finished dual B.S. degrees at USC, where I worked on multilingual speech with Prof. Shrikanth Narayanan at SAIL, and spent summer 2026 at Apple building simulation tooling for satellite connectivity. Outside of research, I enjoy exploring new places ☀︎

Research

Current focus · CMU WavLab × Amazon AGI

Target-Speaker Speech Restoration

Generatively recovering one speaker from a damaged two-party phone call, given only a short sample of their voice. As the lead student researcher, I train models on 7.8K calls and built the evaluation suite (WER/cpWER, speaker similarity, wrong-speaker rate, VERSA, paired bootstrap) that benchmarks 9 external systems. On the systems side, SDPA with a custom position-bias backward made encoder training 1.20× faster with 26% less memory.

  • Generative restoration
  • Speaker conditioning
  • Evaluation
  • PyTorch · ESPnet
CMU WavLab

Perceptual Speech Quality

Non-intrusive models that predict how a listener will rate speech from partial audio. ANCHOR located a 4–6 s perceptual context horizon. Since publication, I've built a ~20× faster one-pass variant at equal accuracy and a zero-shot A/B judge within 0.01 SRCC of the top VoiceMOS 2026 entry on URGENT 2026 pairs.

ANCHOR · Interspeech 2026 →
USC SAIL Lab

Multilingual & Code-Switched Speech

Spoken language diarization for conversational, code-switching speech. I study what pre-trained speech encoders already encode about language identity, and built a training-free diagnostic that ranks when fine-tuning a lightweight probe will pay off.

Preprint · JASA-EL submission →
CMU WavLab

Prosody, Stance & Pragmatic Intent

HuBERT- and prosody-based English–Spanish speech-to-speech translation for the Interspeech 2026 S2ST Challenge, built to carry stance, emotion, and dialog function across languages, so the output keeps what the speaker meant and not only what they said.

Open Source · ESPnet

Reproducible Speech Tooling

Merged contributions to ESPnet (16k+ stars): the Sidon speech-restoration recipe (41 of 52 commits in #6636: GPU-side SSL feature extraction, vocoder stages, XEUS backbone), a >20× speaker-embedding speedup, and new TTS recipes.

ANCHOR: Autoregressive Non-intrusive Chunk-Ordered Refinement for Joint Multi-Resolution Speech Quality Modeling

Zhuoyan Tao, Jiatong Shi, Hye-jin Shim, Shinji Watanabe
Proc. Interspeech 2026, pp. 5672–5677

Extends ARECHO (NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight) to predict speech quality before an utterance finishes. Dual-resolution query tokens with chunk-first decoding in a shared Transformer decoder cut PLCMOS MAE by 48% on 2-second prefixes and locate a 4 to 6 second perceptual context horizon.

003

Off-target posterior mass: A training-free diagnostic for fine-tuning spoken language diarization probes

Zhuoyan Tao, Aditya Kommineni, Shrikanth Narayanan
Submitted to JASA Express Letters · arXiv:2611.13430

Introduces off-target posterior mass (OTPM), a training-free diagnostic computed from language-ID posteriors that ranks how much spoken language diarization probes gain from fine-tuning pre-trained speech encoders (Whisper, MMS, classical LID). Pre-registered on unseen language pairs and tested on two held-out real-world corpora (DISPLACE; MUCS Hindi–English code-switching), where observed gains fell within the frozen 95% prediction interval.
