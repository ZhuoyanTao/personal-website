Interspeech 2026 · Oral

ANCHOR — speech quality from partial audio

Zhuoyan Tao, Jiatong Shi, Hye-jin Shim, Shinji Watanabe

Predicts how listeners will rate speech before the utterance finishes, with 21–35% lower PLCMOS error than ARECHO on 2-second prefixes.

PDF ↗︎ Try the demo ↗︎
Title
ANCHOR: Autoregressive Non-intrusive Chunk-Ordered Refinement for Joint Multi-Resolution Speech Quality Modeling
Venue
Proc. Interspeech 2026, pp. 5672–5677
Summary
Streaming and generative systems need quality estimates from partial audio, but existing predictors assume the full utterance. Extending ARECHO (NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight), ANCHOR models chunk- and utterance-level quality in one autoregressive decoder with dual-resolution query tokens, and shows that judgments converge after a 4–6 s perceptual context horizon.

JASA-EL · Under review

When does language-ID fine-tuning help?

Zhuoyan Tao, Aditya Kommineni, Shrikanth Narayanan

A single training-free number, computed from language-ID posteriors, that forecasts whether fine-tuning a diarization probe will pay off.

PDF ↗︎
Title
Off-target posterior mass: A training-free diagnostic for fine-tuning spoken language diarization probes
Venue
Submitted to JASA Express Letters · arXiv:2611.13430
Summary
Off-target posterior mass (OTPM) correlates with probe gains at 0.56 on unseen synthetic bilingual data and 0.23 on real-world corpora (DISPLACE; MUCS Hindi–English code-switching). The evaluation was pre-registered on unseen language pairs and run across Whisper, MMS, and classical language-ID encoders.
